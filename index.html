Engineering projects

SmartFarm 360

A small-scale automated farming system built with C++, an Arduino board and a soil moisture sensor.

It switches a water pump on through a relay when the soil is dry and off once it is moist enough, cutting water waste and manual work.

Automatic Medical Dispenser

An automated medication dispenser that makes medicine management easier for patients.

Designed for reliability and ease of use, so patients get the right medication at the right time with minimal help.
